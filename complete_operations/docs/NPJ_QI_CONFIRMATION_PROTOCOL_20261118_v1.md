- Follow a physical input with coherence across spin sectors, $|\psi\rangle=(|D_4^2\rangle+|s_{12}\rangle|00\rangle_{34})/\sqrt2$, $|s_{12}\rangle=(|01\rangle-|10\rangle)/\sqrt2$.
