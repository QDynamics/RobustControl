# Full-range training sensitivity and coherent code leakage

18 November 2026. This protocol freezes a new numerical experiment before generating its starts, optimizing controls, scoring any new sample, or evaluating development-code leakage. The physical circuit and legacy control arrays were specified previously. The two analyses below have different inputs and outcomes.

## Physical problem

Use the four Dicke states of weights $N,N-1,N-2,N-3$ as the ordered two-qubit code $00,01,10,11$. One ideal gate acts as $e^{i\pi/4}\mathrm{CNOT}$ on the code and as identity on its entire orthogonal complement. At $N=4,5$ propagate all spin sectors and multiplicities, equivalently the complete $2^N$-dimensional register. The coherent error $D$ is half the diamond norm of the difference of the ideal and actual channels, with arbitrary register/reference input and all physical outputs available for subsequent operations. There is no signal, dephasing, measurement, or postselection in these analyses.

Use the signed pulse pairs, physical slot clock, and parameterization of the register-depth pilot (48 pairs per gate for $N=4$, 72 for $N=5$). Within a pair apply $\exp[-i(1+y)\chi\sum_{a<b}Z_aZ_b]$ then $\exp[-i(1+x)\theta J_\phi]$. The same $x,y$ are held fixed during the entire circuit. Impose $|\theta|\le\pi$, $|\phi|\le3\pi$, $|v|\le0.6$, $\chi=v-\bar v$; thus $|\chi|\le1.2$. The common fixed slots use $t_0=1/g_{\max}$, $\Omega_{\max}/g_{\max}=10$, response time $0.02t_0$, minimum slot $0.04t_0$, and interslot switching $0.001t_0$. Count switches between gates. Input preparation, measurement and reset fall outside the coherent gate clock. Apply the original deterministic azimuth shifts $\pi(\sqrt2 j^2+\sqrt3 j)\bmod2\pi$, $j=0,\ldots,31$, to every rotation of each successive gate; no extra physical rotations. The same fixed controls serve all offsets and depths.

## Optimizer sensitivity

1. Use four fresh PCG64 starts per width, seeds $6112300+N,6112400+N,6112500+N,6112600+N$. Draw, in order, each full vector $\theta\sim U[-1.6,1.6)$, $\phi\sim U[-\pi,\pi)$, $v\sim U[-0.45,0.45)$. Share the same nominal fit (up to 100 one-offset calls) between the two arms of a pair.
2. Train at 17 offsets in $[-0.01,0.01]^2$: center, four corners, four axial endpoints $(\pm0.01,0),(0,\pm0.01)$, and eight interior offsets obtained by all sign choices and both coordinate orders of $(0.003,0.007)$. Weight the complex full-register Frobenius residuals by the square root of each spin multiplicity divided by $\sqrt{2^N}$, equally across offsets. Both finite optimizers use these same offsets and the same residual, control bounds and analytic pulse-parameter Jacobian. The correction stage uses the earlier nominal-plus-derivative Frobenius objective, central differences at $\pm2\times10^{-5}$ on each axis, derivative weight 0.12, with five propagated offsets per parameter trial.
3. Pre-allocate exactly 1,360 elementary offset propagator/Jacobian evaluations per distinct spin representation to each search. The direct path receives at most 80 seventeen-offset trials. The correction-initialized path receives at most 85 five-offset trials followed by at most 55 seventeen-offset trials ($5\times85+17\times55=17\times80$). Caps are fixed, with no padding or transfer of unused work. Both arms receive up to eight additional single-offset nominal restoration calls, giving 1,368 per-arm calls after the shared nominal fit. Record stage-specific actual calls, numbers of distinct block calls, reasons and wall times, including early stopping. Timing is measured separately from these comparable caps. Use binary64/complex128, single-thread BLAS, SciPy bounded trust-region least squares with $f,x,g$ tolerances $10^{-13}$ and the previously evaluated lowest squared residual retained. Use evaluation caps for the fits. Restore using the existing minimum-norm bounded Newton projection, retaining singular values above $10^{-8}$ of the largest, freezing box-violating coordinates, backtracking and stopping at residual norm $10^{-11}$, eight trials, or seven seconds. A failed restoration keeps its actual residual and status.
4. Each arm ranks the same four candidate positions by its maximum **single-gate** training-set $D$ among those with center $D\le10^{-6}$. The positions, also used to break exact ties, are shared nominal, midpoint, finite end, restored end. The direct midpoint is the lowest objective seen through the first 25 seventeen-offset trials (425 calls); on early exit use the incumbent at exit. The seeded midpoint is its correction-stage incumbent (up to 425 calls). Assess both midpoints with the same nominal eligibility rule. Separately record whether the seeded midpoint also meets the earlier first-order criterion (both full-input gain derivative operator norms $\le10^{-5}$). This extra diagnostic does not select the candidate. Score each of four positions once using the same 17 forward offsets per arm (68 score offsets including the center, without Jacobians). If no candidate qualifies, record a failed arm and no paired win. Include nominal fallbacks, unsuccessful restoration and every non-improvement.
5. Reserve **80 distinct test settings**. Take the first 64 ordered independent pairs from `Generator(PCG64(6112700)).uniform(-0.01,0.01,(64,2))`. Add 16 boundary points obtained by fixing either coordinate at $\pm0.01$ and setting the other to $-0.00875,-0.00375,0.00375,0.00875$. These positions are shared across starts and widths. Check all 80 are mutually distinct and disjoint from the 17 training settings, the near-zero correction stencil, and both earlier $5\times5$ and $9\times9$ validation grids. Freeze the selected label of each arm before examining its held-out scores. Primary endpoint for each arm is maximum $D$ across these 80 samples at depth 32. Secondary endpoints are the maxima and all point scores at depths 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16, plus separate interior/edge maxima. Report the signed paired difference (direct minus seeded), counts by width and numerical ties at absolute difference $\le10^{-8}$. Retain the 1% reference as a sampled comparison. This finite set and four starts per width support a sensitivity analysis, without a continuous calibration bound or inference about global optima.

## Development-control leakage

At each width use the archived development seed $6111800+N$ only. Compare its nominal and raw corrected controls from `runs/npj_qi_register_depth_20261118_v1/` and its restored direct/seeded controls from `runs/npj_qi_register_depth_projection_20261118_v1/`. At each of the original 25 Cartesian offsets $\{-0.01,-0.005,0,0.005,0.01\}^2$ and each depth 1, 4, 16, 32 of the original circuit compute $L=\|(I-P)U_{1:M}V_{\rm code}\|_{\rm op}^2$, where the four code columns of $V_{\rm code}$ are orthonormal and $P=V_{\rm code}V_{\rm code}^\dagger$. This is the largest leakage probability over normalized code inputs, with the actual coherent circuit and no renormalization. Compute separately $\|(P_{\rm sym}-P)U_{1:M}V_{\rm code}\|_{\rm op}^2$ and $\|(I-P_{\rm sym})U_{1:M}V_{\rm code}\|_{\rm op}^2$, using every Dicke weight in $P_{\rm sym}$. The maxima of different quadratic forms need not be additive. Collective coherent dynamics preserve total spin, so the second quantity should vanish within roundoff. Retain all 800 point/depth/method records and report sampled maxima separately from the full-input channel metric.

## Audit and records

Replay every selected control at every test point in the independent computational-basis implementation `tools/npj_qi_independent_audit_20261118_v1.py`, which constructs tensor products of one-qubit rotations and computational-basis ZZ phases and evaluates half-diamond distance with the semicircle/chord criterion. Compare against the spin-block fit implementation, which uses the largest spectral gap. For leakage use the same laboratory-basis word and the projectors constructed directly from bit-string Hamming weights; cross-check against projection of the symmetric-spin block onto the unused Dicke weights. Require absolute discrepancies $\le10^{-8}$ for channel distance and leakage probability, and outside-symmetric leakage $\le10^{-20}$. An audit discrepancy is recorded as an audit failure. Store parameters, input/output SHA-256, times, versions, stopping reasons, score tables and scope in a fresh exclusive run directory. No reranking or additional starts after validation. Full replay reconstructs the controls and all diagnostics. A scores-only replay may use the saved controls and frozen labels for metric and leakage verification.
