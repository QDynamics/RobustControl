The pilot showed that eight independent antithetic pairs leave 21 of 42 complete-task comparisons unresolved under its exploratory rule. This motivates a uniform confirmation of **every** candidate and **every** registered parameter point, not targeted sampling of favorable cells.

Freeze the existing eight-member library and all 42 alpha/gamma conditions unchanged. No new controls, optimization, library selection, noise domain, metric, actuator parameters, or ODE tolerance is permitted in this confirmation. Use 128 points: 64 new independent uniform eight-coordinate draws with PCG64 seed 12092722, followed by their negatives. This set is disjoint from training and pilot sets. Evaluate all 43,008 candidate/parameter/sample channels at the already accepted 64 substeps, and all previously registered witness conditions.

Use the mean entanglement infidelity and the same input/first-order eligibility filters. For each cell, compare the lowest mean to the runner-up using its 64 paired differences. Use a two-sided Student interval with 63 degrees of freedom and a Bonferroni critical value covering all k(k-1)/2 fixed candidate pairs within that cell, where k is the eligible library size. Mark the comparison unresolved if this interval includes zero or the mean gap is <=4e-7. This is an approximate within-cell diagnostic relying on the sampling distribution of means; do not claim exact coverage or simultaneous coverage across the 42 conditions. Preserve pilot results and pilot figures separately. Final figures use this entire new confirmation set; report pilot and confirmation counts explicitly, including disagreements and unresolved comparisons. Never combine the two samples selectively.
